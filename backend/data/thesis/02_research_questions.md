# Principal research questions

1. What are the driving ideas of current international support for Chavismo?
2. As a contrast to these ideas, what then drives people to leave Venezuela?
3. How do Venezuelans respond and react to this non-Venezuelan support for the government / Chavismo?

Supporting questions explored through interviews and ethnography include:
- How do solidarity activists define “solidarity” with the Bolivarian Revolution?
- How do diaspora Venezuelans frame the crisis, emigration, and the legitimacy of Maduro’s government?
- Where do both groups locate blame (sanctions vs. mismanagement)?
- How is the notion of el pueblo used to claim democratic legitimacy?
